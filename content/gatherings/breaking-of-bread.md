On the Lord's Day we remember the Lord Jesus Christ in the Breaking of Bread, as He asked His own to do.

> This do in remembrance of me.  
> — Luke 22:19 KJV

This is a worship meeting. There is no appointed minister. Brothers may give thanks, read the Scriptures, and suggest a hymn, as the Lord leads.

Believers in the Lord Jesus Christ who are sound in life and doctrine are welcome to share in the emblems. Visitors who are not breaking bread are warmly welcome to sit with us.

Please [confirm the time](/contact/) before your first visit.
