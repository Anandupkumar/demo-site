Following the Breaking of Bread we have a time of ministry from the Word of God. The Scriptures are read, and a brother may open them for the help of the saints.

We use the Authorised (King James) Version. You are welcome to bring your own Bible.

This meeting is for believers and for any who wish to hear the Word. Children sit with their families.

Please [confirm the time](/contact/) before your first visit.
