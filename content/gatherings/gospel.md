Each Lord's Day evening we preach the gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ.

This meeting is especially for those who do not yet know Him as Saviour, and for any who would hear again the old, old story. There is singing, the reading of the Scriptures, and a gospel address.

You do not need to know anyone. Come as you are. There is no collection required of visitors.

Read more of [the gospel we preach](/the-gospel/), or [plan your visit](/visit/).
