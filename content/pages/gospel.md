---

God loves the world, and He has spoken in His Son.

> For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.  
> — John 3:16 KJV

## The need

We are sinners before a holy God. Our own goodness cannot make us right with Him.

> For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God.  
> — Romans 3:23 KJV

## The Saviour

The Lord Jesus Christ is the eternal Son of God. He became man, lived without sin, and died at Calvary in the sinner's place. God raised Him from the dead.

> But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.  
> — Romans 5:8 KJV

**Jesus Christ is our God, our Saviour, and our life.**

## The way

Salvation is not by church membership, nor by works, but by faith in the Lord Jesus Christ.

> Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved.  
> — Acts 16:31 KJV
