title: Plan your visit
description: What to expect when you come to Manchester Apostolic Brethren Church in Atherton.
---

You are warmly welcome. We know that walking into an unfamiliar meeting can feel uncertain. These notes are here so your first visit is as straightforward as we can make it.

## When and where

We meet in Atherton, in the heart of Greater Manchester. Lord's Day gatherings are in the morning and evening; there is also a midweek meeting for prayer and the Word.

Please [see our gatherings](/gatherings/) for indicative times. Confirm the time of the meeting you hope to attend before you travel — especially on bank holidays.

## What to expect

Come as you are. You will find a simple hall, open Bibles, and hymn singing. The Breaking of Bread is a worship meeting for believers; visitors are welcome to sit with us. The gospel meeting is especially for any who would hear the good news of the Lord Jesus Christ.

Meetings last about an hour. There is no collection plate passed to visitors.

## Dress

There is no dress code. Wear what you would wear to a respectful family gathering. Some sisters among us cover their heads in the meetings, according to 1 Corinthians 11; this is not required of visitors.

## Children

Children are welcome in the gatherings. We do not operate a separate Sunday school during the Breaking of Bread. If you have questions about a child's needs, please [contact us](/contact/) beforehand and we will do what we can to help.

## Getting here

Atherton is served by local buses and is a short journey from the wider Manchester area. Street parking is usually available nearby. If you would like directions or to be met at the door, send a message through the [contact page](/contact/).

## Accessibility

We want every visitor to be able to join us. If you have a mobility, hearing, or other need, please tell us in advance so we can prepare. We will be honest about what the building allows and will help as far as we can.

## Let us know you are coming

You do not need to book. If you would like someone to watch for you, or you have a question before you arrive, [write to us](/contact/). We will be glad to hear from you.
