We gather as brethren in the Name of the Lord Jesus Christ. The times below are indicative placeholders and should be confirmed before your first visit.

There is no charge to attend, and you do not need to book. [Plan your visit](/visit/) if you are coming for the first time.

Offerings, when given, are a matter of fellowship among those who gather — not a requirement of visitors.
