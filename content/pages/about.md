We are a local, independent Christian church in the heart of Atherton, Manchester. We are not a denomination, and we do not gather around a human leader. We gather simply as brethren — brothers and sisters in Christ — in the Name of the Lord Jesus Christ.

**Jesus Christ is our God, our Saviour, and our life.** Because of Him, we are a people of Hope.

## What we believe

We believe the Holy Scriptures to be the inspired Word of God, and we read them in the Authorised (King James) Version. We confess the Lord Jesus Christ as the eternal Son of God, who became man, died for our sins, rose again, and is coming again.

We believe that salvation is by grace, through faith in His finished work at Calvary — not by our works, nor by belonging to a particular company of Christians.

## How we gather

We meet as an assembly of believers gathered unto His Name, according to the pattern we find in the New Testament. There is no appointed clergyman. Brothers who are exercised may take part in prayer, praise, and ministry, as the Lord leads.

The Breaking of Bread — the Lord's Supper — is the centre of our Lord's Day gathering. We also have ministry of the Word, a gospel meeting, and a midweek time of prayer and Bible study.

## Leadership

Oversight is in the hands of local brethren who seek to shepherd the flock. We do not promote personalities. If you wish to speak with someone before you visit, please [contact us](/contact/) and we will gladly arrange it.

## A people of Hope

We look for the coming of the Lord Jesus Christ. Until then, we desire to walk in simplicity, to keep His Word, and to welcome any who would come and see.
